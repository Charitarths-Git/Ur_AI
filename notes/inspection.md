## Q1 — Do the two libraries agree on tokenization? Where do they differ?

**Short answer:** They agree on x0/y0 positions to within ~1 pt for most tokens, but differ significantly in two ways:

1. **Token boundaries:** PyMuPDF splits `(2025年３月31日)` into three tokens —
   `'(2025'` (x0=297.84), `'年３月31'` (x0=322.68), `'日)'` (x0=363.23) — because it
   splits on whitespace and some CJK-to-ASCII boundaries. pdfplumber keeps it as a
   single token `'(2025年３月31日) '` (x0=297.84). This matters for the date row header:
   PyMuPDF produces 6 tokens for the two period headings; pdfplumber produces 2.

2. **Trailing spaces:** pdfplumber appends a trailing space to every word
   (e.g. `'現金及び預金 '` vs `'現金及び預金'`). Strip before comparing.

3. **Whitespace tokens:** pdfplumber emits pure-space tokens `' '` for column separator
   positions (x0=164.64, x0=397.44, x0=522.48) that PyMuPDF omits entirely. These
   correspond to rule/separator positions in the drawings (see Q7).

4. **Word order in the output list:** PyMuPDF delivers words in internal stream order
   (page footer `- 4 -` appears first, before the title). pdfplumber delivers in
   visual reading order (top-to-bottom, left-to-right).

5. **Word counts:** PyMuPDF=132, pdfplumber=144 (extra space tokens account for the
   difference of 12).

**Conclusion:** Both libraries agree on the position and text of substantive tokens.
For row reconstruction, PyMuPDF is preferred (no spurious space tokens, slightly
cleaner bbox). pdfplumber is a useful cross-check for boundary cases.

**Is indentation alone sufficient?** Largely yes — but with important caveats:

1. **Duplicate labels at the same level:** `リース資産` appears twice at x0=101 (once
   under 有形固定資産, once under 無形固定資産). `その他` appears three times at x0=101.
   `減価償却累計額` appears four times at x0=114. Indentation gives the level but not
   the specific parent — parent assignment requires context from the preceding Level-2
   section header.

2. **Section-header rows with no numbers:** `有形固定資産` (x0=88) has no numbers and
   acts as a sub-category label. `無形固定資産` and `投資その他の資産` are the same.
   These rows serve as parents but are themselves children of `固定資産`.

3. **Subtotal/net rows have distinct naming patterns** that cross-check indentation:
   - Labels ending in `合計` are subtotals of the nearest same-level group above.
   - Labels ending in `（純額）` are net values (gross − 減価償却累計額).
   - `減価償却累計額` rows are always Level 4 deductions.

**Conclusion:** Indentation gives the level reliably. To assign the correct parent path
for duplicate-label items, a rule must track the most recent Level-2 (x0≈88) header
seen above each row. Text-based rules (合計, （純額）, 減価償却累計額) are needed as
cross-checks but not as the primary method.

## Q9 — Anything surprising or risky for later phases?

1. **Page footer comes first in PyMuPDF word stream:** The `- 4 -` tokens (`'-'`, `'4'`,
   `'-'`) have y0=818.95 but appear at indices 0–2 in the raw PyMuPDF word list (before
   `（訂正後）` at index 3). This is a stream-order artifact. Always sort by y0 before
   processing.

2. **142 shaded (light-blue) rects — not alternating rows, but all column-pair cells:**
   Each data row has two numeric cells shaded, creating two rects per row for the value
   columns. This means shading cannot be used to distinguish header rows from data rows.

3. **Subtotal rows are spaced 12.72 pt apart vs 12.0 pt for data rows** — the slight
   difference comes from the rule line (0.72 pt) above subtotal rows consuming space.
   This means y-spacing alone is insufficient to distinguish subtotal rows; use the black
   thin-rect rule marker instead.

4. **`（純額）` label rows (e.g. `建物及び構築物（純額）`) sit at Level 4 (x0=114)**
   alongside `減価償却累計額`. Both are children of the gross asset row above. A
   parser must handle the case where a net row is not simply a line-item but a computed
   value (gross − accumulated depreciation).

5. **`△0` in the current-year column of `貸倒引当金`** — represents a near-zero
   negative number. A normalization rule of "strip △ and negate" produces -0, which
   equals 0. The rounding-tolerance check must accept this.

6. **`－` (nil) in `建設仮勘定` prev-year column** (x0=388.44, inside the prev-year
   range 347–397) — the nil marker is right-aligned to the same x1≈397.4 as numbers.
   It must be parsed as "no value / zero" rather than as a label fragment.

7. **Font name is `MS-Mincho` for body text** — a common Japanese serif font. Font size
   is uniformly 9.0 pt for all body rows. This means font-size variation cannot be used
   to detect headers (headers use the same 9.0 pt body font; only the title line uses
   10.56 pt).

8. **pdfplumber emits separator-position space tokens** at x0=164.64, 397.44, 522.48 —
   these correspond to the column-divider rule positions. Strip whitespace-only tokens
   before use.
